# Story
Rural hospitals in the Upper Midwest keep towns running, and closures make news. I wanted to see what public cost-report data shows about financial strain.
I pulled CMS FY2023 cost reports and kept one full-year report per hospital. My first margin numbers were absurd (losses of thousands of percent) because of tiny revenue denominators, so I added a $1M revenue floor and a margin range check.
Then I counted six simple, explainable financial warning signs instead of building a black-box model. The surprise: the region looks better than the national picture overall, but Wyoming and North Dakota have a high share of hospitals with negative margins.

> **Caveat:** Hospitals in larger health systems (e.g. Sanford, Monument, Avera) may show low cash because the parent system holds it. This is a screening score from public CMS FY2023 cost reports, not a prediction or a judgment of any hospital.

Other limits: one year only, and the CMS rural flag includes some big city hospitals.
