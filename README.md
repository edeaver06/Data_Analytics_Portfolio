### Portfolio Projects

#### Python

##### 📞 Google Fiber Support & FCR Analysis
* **Code:** [`projects/google-fiber/`](projects/google-fiber/)
* **Goal:** Identify root-cause drivers behind repeat call center volume and 7-day First-Contact Resolution (FCR) failures across 3 markets.
* **Description:** Processed Q1 customer support telemetry logs using Pandas and Seaborn. Built company-wide weighted summaries and visual figures isolating workload concentration.
* **Skills & Tools:** Python, Pandas, Matplotlib, Seaborn, ETL Pipeline.
* **Results:** Proved that **Wi-Fi & Internet** and **Technical Troubleshooting** drive over **80% of all repeat calls**, recommending 1-click diagnostic tools for frontline agents.

##### 📦 Olist E-Commerce Marketplace Intelligence
* **Code:** [`projects/olist-ecommerce/`](projects/olist-ecommerce/)
* **Goal:** Analyze marketplace order fulfillment, delivery delay impacts on customer review scores, and seller performance.
* **Description:** Linked relational datasets across 100,000+ Brazilian e-commerce transactions to evaluate regional logistics bottlenecks and customer retention.
* **Skills & Tools:** Python, SQL, Relational Schema Design, Data Cleaning.
* **Results:** Isolated regional freight delay thresholds that trigger 1-star reviews, providing logistics routing strategies to mitigate customer churn.

#### R & Machine Learning

##### 🎯 Operational Risk Strategic Audit Prioritization
* **Report:** [View RPubs Analysis](https://rpubs.com/slammerjammer/1422063) | **Code:** [`projects/operational-risk/`](projects/operational-risk/)
* **Goal:** Replace static audit schedules with a predictive risk-scoring model to optimize resource allocation.
* **Description:** Trained a Random Forest classifier in R to isolate systemic risk signatures across operational units.
* **Skills & Tools:** R, Random Forest, Tidyverse, ggplot2, Cross-Validation.
* **Results:** Concentrated 70% of field audit resources onto high-risk Tier 3 clusters where operational failure is most likely.

##### 🤖 Forensic Fraud Audit: AI Scam Detection
* **Report:** [View RPubs Analysis](https://rpubs.com/slammerjammer/1423363) | **Code:** [`projects/ai-scam-detection/`](projects/ai-scam-detection/)
* **Goal:** Detect robotic LLM text signatures and linguistic entropy in automated financial scams.
* **Description:** Built a forensic audit model comparing word length, predictability scores, and complexity between human speech and synthetic scam lures.
* **Skills & Tools:** R, Random Forest, Feature Engineering, Threat Forensics.
* **Results:** Identified structural predictability spikes in LLM-generated lures, creating an automated gateway filter for fraud mitigation.

##### 🚲 Cyclistic Bike-Share Subscription Conversion
* **Report:** [View RPubs Analysis](https://rpubs.com/slammerjammer/1371255) | **Code:** [`projects/cyclistic/`](projects/cyclistic/)
* **Goal:** Evaluate behavioral usage disparities between casual riders and annual subscribers.
* **Description:** Conducted exploratory data analysis and hypothesis testing on trip duration, day-of-week trends, and station usage.
* **Skills & Tools:** R, dplyr, ggplot2, Hypothesis Testing.
* **Results:** Delivered targeted marketing recommendations to convert high-frequency casual commuters into annual subscribers.
